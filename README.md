# Android 17 Contact Picker in Flutter: time to replace READ_CONTACTS

Android 17 introduces a new Contact Picker that lets apps access user-selected contacts without the READ_CONTACTS permission. In this Flutter tutorial, we’ll look at Google Play’s new Contacts Permissions policy, when you need to replace READ_CONTACTS, and how to implement the Android Contact Picker using ACTION_PICK_CONTACTS and ContactsPickerSessionContract. We’ll also cover targetSdk 37, READ_CONTACTS maxSdkVersion, and what to do if your app still needs full contacts access and requires a Play Console declaration.
